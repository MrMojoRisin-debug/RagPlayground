# Northwind Beacon - Product FAQ

*Sample document - Northwind Labs (fictional company). Delete anytime.*

## What is the Northwind Beacon?
The Beacon is a battery-powered indoor air-quality sensor. It measures CO2, temperature,
humidity, and fine particulate matter (PM2.5), and reports readings to the Northwind app.

## How long does the battery last?
A full charge lasts about **18 months** at the default 10-minute reporting interval. Switching
to the 1-minute "live" interval reduces battery life to roughly **3 months**. The Beacon
charges over USB-C in about 2 hours.

## Which connectivity options are supported?
The Beacon connects over **Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only)** or **Thread**. 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks are not
supported. Bluetooth is used only for initial setup.

## How do I reset the device?
Hold the top button for **10 seconds** until the ring light flashes amber three times. This
erases Wi-Fi credentials and pairing data but keeps the sensor calibration.

## What is the warranty?
Every Beacon comes with a **2-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects. Battery
degradation below 70% capacity within the warranty period is also covered.

## Does the Beacon work with smart-home platforms?
Yes: it supports **Matter**, so it works with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Home
Assistant.

## What does the ring light colour mean?
- Green: air quality good (CO2 below 800 ppm)
- Yellow: moderate (800-1200 ppm)
- Red: poor (above 1200 ppm) - consider ventilating
