# Refunds and Returns Policy

*Sample document - Northwind Labs (fictional company). Delete anytime.*

## Return window
Customers may return any hardware product within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund.
Software licenses can be refunded within **14 days of purchase** if the license key has not
been activated on more than one device.

## Condition of returned items
Hardware must be returned with all original accessories. Items with physical damage caused by
the customer are eligible for a partial refund of 50% at the discretion of the returns team.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after the
returned item is received at our Rotterdam warehouse. Store credit is issued instantly if the
customer prefers it, and store credit carries a 10% bonus.

## Exceptions
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
- Custom-engraved Northwind Beacon units are non-refundable.
- Enterprise orders above 50 units follow the terms in the customer's master services agreement.

## Shipping costs
Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other returns the customer pays return
shipping, which is deducted from the refund (flat fee of EUR 9.50).
