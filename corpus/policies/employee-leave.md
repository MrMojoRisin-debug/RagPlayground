# Employee Leave Policy

*Sample document - Northwind Labs (fictional company). Delete anytime.*

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue **25 days of PTO per year**, credited monthly (about 2.08 days per
month). Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on contracted hours.

Up to **5 unused PTO days** may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
expire on **31 March** if not used.

## Requesting leave
PTO requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least **two weeks in advance** for
absences longer than three consecutive days. Shorter absences need only manager approval in
the portal before the day off.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped. A doctor's note is required for absences
longer than **three consecutive working days**.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive **20 weeks** of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive
**12 weeks** of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the
child's birth or adoption.

## Company holidays
The office is closed between 24 December and 1 January. These days do not count against PTO.
